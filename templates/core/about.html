TIIH operates under Taita Taveta University to support innovation, business incubation, entrepreneurship, mentoring, training and the commercialization of innovations.

The hub's purpose is to help students, staff and community members move thoughtfully from problem discovery and ideation toward product development, stronger venture models and appropriate market connections.
